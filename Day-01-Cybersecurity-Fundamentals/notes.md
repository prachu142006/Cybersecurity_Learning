# Day 01 - Cybersecurity Fundamentals

## What is Cybersecurity?
Cybersecurity protects systems, networks and data
from cyberattacks and unauthorized access.

## What is SOC?
SOC stands for Security Operations Center.
It monitors and investigates security incidents.

## SOC Analyst Responsibilities
- Monitoring
- Detection
- Investigation
- Incident Response
- Reporting

## CIA Triad
1. Confidentiality - Protect data privacy.
2. Integrity - Maintain data accuracy.
3. Availability - Keep services accessible.

## Important Terms
- Threat
- Vulnerability
- Risk
- Attack Surface
- Malware
- Firewall
- Authentication
- Authorization
